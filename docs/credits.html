Credits

A prototype for learning Mandarin. Every sentence credits its source underneath it. The data behind it:

  • ivankra/hsk30: official HSK 3.0 word list and grammar outline (MIT).
  • CC-CEDICT: definitions and character meanings (CC BY-SA 4.0).
  • Tatoeba: everyday sentences and their human English translations (CC BY 2.0 FR). Each sentence links to its Tatoeba page.
  • Chinese Wikipedia: the Scholar sentences, from featured articles (CC BY-SA 4.0). Each sentence links to its article. English for these is offline machine translation (Argos Translate) and may contain errors.
  • complete-hsk-vocabulary: word frequency (MIT).
  • Grammar example sentences come from the Language Player Chinese grammar chart (a third-party course); the 7–9 band translations there are unreviewed machine translation.

Word segmentation, pinyin and script conversion: jieba, pypinyin, OpenCC.
